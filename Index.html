01 / Approach

Strategy is only useful when something ships.

My work sits between growth marketing and web development. I don't just plan campaigns — I write the funnels, the SEO, the internal software and the operating workflows behind them.

I use AI tools such as Claude and ChatGPT to accelerate research, drafting, analysis and first-pass implementation. I make the architectural calls, validate the work and own what actually ships.

Most of my strongest work starts with imperfect conditions: no established channel, no system, limited traction or a process that is too manual to scale. Starting from zero is a problem I am comfortable owning.

The goal is not to create more marketing activity. It is to build a system where acquisition, conversion, delivery and measurement reinforce one another.

Situation

A new exchange with no local users

Bitlink, a crypto exchange founded in Asia, chose Nigeria as its first African market. It needed local users and visibility fast, in a market crowded with established exchanges.

Task

What I owned

Activation

Campus adoption

Get students to sign up on the exchange, not just hear about it.

Events

Lagos launch

Organise the official launch event in Lagos.

Growth

Business development

Build local relationships for Bitlink in Nigeria.

Action

Campus activation across five universities

We ran activations at UNILAG, the University of Abuja, LASU, Babcock University and Yaba College of Technology. At each campus the aim was sign-ups on the exchange first, then attendance at in-person sessions.

Campus outreach→Sign-up on the exchange→In-person activation→Community

Alongside the campus work, I organised the Lagos launch event in December 2023, where Bitlink’s leadership presented its plans for Nigeria and Africa.

Result

Signups first, then attendance

  • About 250 students signed up on the exchange across the five campuses
  • About 21% of them came to the in-person activations
  • The Lagos launch was covered by TechCabal and BusinessDay

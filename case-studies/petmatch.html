A free way for Nigerian pet owners to find mates for their pets

PetMatch is a free, simple directory that helps dog and cat owners across Nigeria find nearby mates for their pets and connect directly. No agents, no fees.

Situation

Pet owners relied on agents and word of mouth

Owners looking for a mate for their dog or cat usually go through agents or ask around. PetMatch lets them connect directly, locally and for free.

Action

Supply first, then community

Listing before finding

A match is only possible once pets are listed, so the “List a Pet” flow was built and optimised before “Find a Mate”. Listing is the entry point.

Low-friction outreach

Listings grow through calls followed by WhatsApp messages to owners and local pet businesses, with a simple yes or no ask: no cost and no commitment.

Naija Pet Owners

A free Instagram and WhatsApp community (@naija_pet_owners) gives pet owners a place to talk and feeds new listings into the platform. Content runs daily on a repeating weekly loop of themes.

What comes next

A partner programme for pet stores is being designed: the first 50 approved partners get a free website, an official PetMatch scan code for their store and a partner badge on the platform. Matched owners will be able to meet at a partner store nearby, or wherever they choose.

Result

Early, honest numbers

Listings have grown to 12, and the community is free to join. This is an early-stage, community-led project: the focus right now is listings, not partnerships or revenue.

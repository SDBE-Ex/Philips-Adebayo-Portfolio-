Situation

A trusted brand with a website that couldn’t sell

The brand has decades in the market and a wide range of fragrances and personal care products sold through retail across the country. Its website showed products as images with no prices, no cart and no checkout, so interest had nowhere to go.

SDBE works as strategic digital partner to the agency that manages the account. I led the website development.

Action

Five changes that made the site a shop

BeforeAfter
A dark, full-screen mood photo with no offer and no buttonA clear promise in the first line, the product range in view, trust badges, and Shop Now and Our Story buttons
Brand photos with product names onlyProduct cards with naira prices and Add to Cart on every product
No way to payLive online card and bank payments at checkout
No delivery optionsA courier aggregator in checkout, with rates by product weight
Duplicate and broken pagesClean-up so every link leads somewhere real

The new first screen answers three questions: what this brand is, why to trust it, and what to do next.

Result

First online sales, and a client ready for the next phase

3 organic online sales in under two months after launch on 25 September 2026, with no ad spend. That is a milestone, not a growth figure: the first orders this brand has taken through its own site.

The client has agreed a second phase: a custom-built site and admin system for stronger security, easier product management and sales insight, keeping the current design. It is planned for the new year.
